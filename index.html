Privacy Policy

for Wordsure, developed by Kseniia Pedersen

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Wordsure (the “App”), developed by Kseniia Pedersen (“we”, “us”, or “our”), handles information when you use the App. In short: the App works on your device and does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

1. Information the App Stores

a) Your setup choices

When you first open the App, you can tell it about yourself so it can pick suitable words. This includes:

  • Whether English is your first language, and the country you are from
  • The language you want translations in
  • The topics you are interested in and your daily goal for new words
  • An estimate of your vocabulary level, based on the quick level test or on your own answers

b) Your learning progress

As you use the App, it remembers:

  • Which words you have started, learned, or marked as known
  • Your answers to exercises and review ratings, used to schedule when a word comes back
  • The days you were active, used for your streak and progress calendar

This information is saved only in the App’s private storage on your device. It does not contain your name, email address, or other direct personal identifiers.

c) What the App does not collect

  • No account, name, email address, or phone number is requested
  • No location, contacts, photos, files, camera, or microphone data
  • No advertising ID, and no advertising of any kind
  • No analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services

2. How the Information Is Used

The information above is used on your device only, to:

  • Choose new words that suit your level and interests
  • Schedule reviews and show your progress
  • Keep your settings between sessions

It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose outside the App.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. The App does not transmit your setup choices or learning progress to us or to any other server.

4. Pronunciation (Text-to-Speech)

When you tap a play button, the App asks your phone’s built-in text-to-speech engine to read a word aloud. The engine is part of your device (for example, the one provided by Google or your phone’s manufacturer). Depending on your device settings, that engine may process the word under its own privacy policy. The App itself does not send the words anywhere.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Your data is kept in the App’s private storage on your device and is protected by your device’s own security
  • Because the App does not send your data anywhere, there is no copy of it on our servers
  • Android may include the App’s data in your device backup. This is controlled by your own device and Google account settings, not by us

6. User Control and Data Deletion

You are in control of your data on your device:

  • In the App, open the Me tab to change your answers, retake the level test, or reset your progress
  • To remove everything, clear the App’s storage in your phone’s settings, or uninstall the App

Since we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you have a question about your data, you can contact us at: madreadbook@gmail.com

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. If a future version of the App adds features that collect or send data, such as accounts or synchronization across devices, we will update this policy before those features begin to collect any data.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: madreadbook@gmail.com
